# Mission

## Why I'm learning this

Bhavik has a **written AI test tomorrow (26 Sep 2026), 30 minutes long**. The
immediate mission is to walk into that test able to write confident,
well-structured short-note answers from memory.

## The three examinable topics

1. **What is Machine Learning + its applications**
2. **Forms of Machine Learning** — Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement
3. **Overfitting and Underfitting**

## Exam format (drives everything)

- Written, ~30 minutes
- "Short notes" style — roughly **5–7 points per answer**
- Questions are unpredictable in *phrasing*: could be a straight definition, a
  "difference between", a "explain with example", or a comparison table
- So: knowledge must be **producible from memory**, not just recognisable

## Success criteria

Bhavik can, unprompted, write 5–7 solid points on any of the three topics —
including a definition, the key distinguishing features, and at least one
concrete example per concept.

## Beyond tomorrow

Passing is the near-term goal, but the deeper aim is that these three topics
become a genuine mental foundation — ML vocabulary he can build on later,
rather than crammed sentences that evaporate after the test.
